Problem & Market Context

The fraud wave is outpacing every defender.

Cybercrime complaints, FIA 2022→24 (+70%); 2024 total per NCCIA. 2022 bar derived from growth rate.
  • 47% of 2024 complaints were financial fraud — the top category (NCCIA)
  • Est. ~$9B lost to internet fraud every year (research est.)
  • ≈220 financial-fraud complaints per day reported; true attempts 10–20× higher (est.)
  • 117M internet users, 45.6% penetration — most are first-time targets (DataReportal 2026)
  • One OTP scam can erase months of a family's income. There is no undo button.
Real Pattern · Real Panic

This is the message that empties savings.

INCOMING SMS · 03XX-XXXXXXX
"Mubarak ho! Apko 25,000 mile hain. OTP bhejein foran warna account band ho jayega."
OTP FORAN ACCOUNT BAND SCORE 96/100 · SCAM
To a 52-year-old mother it reads like luck. To our engine it is a textbook BISP-8171 fraud pattern — flagged in under a second.
Solution

Paste it. Verdict in 3 seconds. In her language.

  • Paste any SMS, WhatsApp forward or screenshot → SCAM · SUSPICIOUS · SAFE
  • Explanation in three languages: English, Roman Urdu, and Nastaliq Urdu
  • Every verdict shows its evidence — the exact trigger words, never a black box
  • The phone speaks the verdict aloud in Urdu — built for low literacy and panic
  • One obvious next action. Clarity beats cleverness.

System Architecture

Three layers. Zero single points of failure.

Why This Architecture Wins

Resilient. Cheap. Conservative by design.

Resilience

503

During rehearsal our cloud endpoint went down mid-test. The app's verdict never changed — 3-second cap plus the on-device engine absorb any backend failure.

Cost Moat

−85%

When the fine-tuned 1.5B becomes Layer 1, ~85% of scans never touch paid inference. Same verdicts, one-eighth the bill.

Accuracy Guarantee

70

Confidence gate: below 70 the message escalates to the bigger model. Unsure always means suspicious — never safe. Every response carries its model_used receipt.

Business Model

Users never pay. Institutions do.

  • B2B white-label verdict API — banks, wallets and telcos plug into the same /analyze contract we demo today
  • B2G & CSR — digital-literacy and fraud-prevention programs with regulators and foundations
  • Freemium family seats — only after scale; never on the core protection
  • Charging the vulnerable is a trust failure. The shield stays free.
